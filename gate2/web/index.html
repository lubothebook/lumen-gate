My tickets

A ticket is a claim on native USDC in the vault. It is not a bridged asset. It is bearer paper: a ticket sent to the wrong address or stolen cannot be clawed back. If Circle freezes the vault, every ticket is affected.

Info

The trust model, on the page that talks to it

We do not say “trustless”. The trust root is Circle’s CCTP attestation. Circle can freeze USDC.

Who verifies

Circle Iris. No attestation, no claim. This page does not operate a bridge.

Who does not custody

GateClaim and BurnRouter hold no funds. The two vaults that do — Battery and Ticket — are live on testnet and hold nothing yet; both report a zero balance on chain, which you can read above.

What is absent

No fake token list, one-click burn, unlimited approval, or third-party watcher.
