Why this belongs on Stellar

Stellar has the rails.
Soroban can enforce the receipt.

Anchors are already Stellar’s bridge to real-world value. Lumen Gate focuses on the missing coordination layer: making a source-chain settlement decision transparent, replay-safe and composable with Stellar assets.

01 / RAILS

Anchor-native by design

Fits the SEP-1/6/24/31 surface instead of asking every anchor to become a validator operator.

02 / CRYPTO

Proofs where money moves

BLS12-381 and BN254 verification are shaped for Soroban’s native cryptographic host functions.

03 / EVIDENCE

Receipts, not promises

Events, transaction hashes, roots and heights become part of the settlement record an operator can inspect.

04 / SAFETY

Failure is a first-class result

Bad signatures, roots, versions, proofs and replayed nonces are deliberately demonstrated as rejected states.

The settlement boundary

One story. Two directions.
Every step accountable.

The source simulator keeps the hackathon reproducible. The Stellar side is intentionally not mocked: the gateway, registry, SAC and RPC receipts are the target system.

01

Lock

Source event commits amount, recipient, height, root and message envelope.

02

Prove

Relayer carries BLS or Groth16 evidence with payload-derived checks.

03

Verify

Finality Registry rejects malformed, stale, mismatched or replayed evidence.

04

Mint on Stellar

Gateway controls the SAC authority only after the registry approves.

05

Burn → unlock

Gateway emits canonical Bytes; the relayer delivers a one-time source unlock.
